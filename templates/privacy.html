Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Here is how we handle your data.

1. Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including your email address when you register and the financial transaction details (amounts, categories, and dates) that you log into your personal tracker.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide the expense tracking service. We use it to generate your spending summaries, category breakdowns, and to manage your user account. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access. Your information is stored securely, and we only retain data for as long as your account remains active.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not currently share your data with third-party services. If we ever integrate with external financial APIs or analytics tools, we will update this policy and notify you.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us via the support email provided in your account settings.
